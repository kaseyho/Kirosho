# Competition requirements

Checked 21 October 2026 against the [overview](https://learn-specs.devpost.com/)
and [official rules](https://learn-specs.devpost.com/rules).

The entry must be a new SPECS Lens created during the submission period, planned
with Devpost Learn and built through CLAD, Lens Studio, and its MCP. It must run
consistently in the desktop preview and match its demonstration.

Submit a public project repository, an English description, and a public YouTube
video showing it working. Keep the video below three minutes. Preserve the
generated `devpost/` documents: the overview requests them even though the rules
describe their inclusion as optional. Disclose incorporated pre-existing work
and comply with third-party licenses.

Deadline: **9 November 2026, 5:00 pm EST**, which is **10 November, 6:00 am SGT**.
Our internal readiness target remains 9 November, 6:00 pm SGT.

Design, implementation, innovation, and presentation receive equal weight.
Eligibility, account declarations, and agreement to the entry rules remain
account-holder decisions. This document records requirements; it does not submit
an entry or certify eligibility.
